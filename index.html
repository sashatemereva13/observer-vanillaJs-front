Observer Pattern Demo

Understanding the Observer Pattern through addEventListener

1. The Subject

The Button (Broadcaster)

The DOM element that maintains a registry of observers and notifies them when events occur.

document.querySelector("#buy-btn")
2. The Observers

Callback Functions (Listeners)

  • notifyUser() - Shows alert
  • logTransaction() - Logs to console

These wait for the subject to notify them.

3. The Communication

Subscription → Notification

Subscribe: addEventListener()
Notify: When clicked, all observers execute
Unsubscribe: removeEventListener()

🔑 Key Concepts
  • Decoupled Communication: The button doesn't know what the observers do—it just notifies them.
  • One-to-Many: One subject can notify multiple observers simultaneously.
  • Automatic Synchronization: All observers are updated instantly when the event fires.
  • Dynamic Management: Observers can be added or removed at runtime.
